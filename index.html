Recording Settings

Resolution

Applies to the canvas-composited output (when a webcam is active). Native screen capture resolution is decided by the browser and the selected source; the values here are targets, not guarantees.

Frame rate

Video bitrate

Higher bitrates produce larger files and may drop frames on slower hardware.

100%
100%

System audio is captured only when your browser offers it with the selected source (tab or screen sharing with “share audio” enabled).

Webcam overlay

When enabled, the screen and webcam are composited on a canvas and recorded as a single stream at the selected resolution/frame rate. When disabled, the native screen track is recorded directly (webcam stays visible in the overlay but is not burned into the video unless composition is on).
